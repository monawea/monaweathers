Hey Coaches,

Have you ever had something unexpected happen that slowed down your business building? That's what happened to me this past week.

Last weekend my mom hurt her back and was admitted to the hospital. She's 81, so injuries can be more serious at her age. I'm happy to say she's doing much better and is heading to a physical rehab center today. I'm so thankful she's okay.

Weeks like this remind me why I'm building an online coaching business. Life gets hard sometimes, and I love having the flexibility to work when I can instead of by a 9 to 5 time clock. Not everything got done this week, and that's okay. I was there for my family, kept my focus on my clients' projects, and kept my business running on my terms.

I'm guessing you have similar reasons for building your coaching or consulting business.

That's a big part of why I'm hosting the Experience to Income cohort. I don't want you to lose these last few months of 2026. You can set up your business in a way that leaves room for family emergencies and the things that matter most.

How? By simplifying your focus and doing the things that matter for long-term growth. So many of us hit the end of the year, get pulled into the holidays, and let our business building fade. When that happens, the new year gets off to a slow start.

This cohort helps you set up the right things, keep going, and get support and encouragement along the way. It's 30 days of intention and action.

Your 2027 plan for a thriving coaching business

  • Get clear on who you help and what you offer so you can say it out loud with confidence
  • Set up the one platform and profile that works for you, then start showing up with a plan
  • Start real conversations with people who already know you, without pitching
  • Learn when and how to make your offer, and make it to the people who are ready
  • Turn what you built this month into a weekly routine you can keep going into the new year
