THE PROBLEM

Your linktree can't hold your business.
A full website is more than anyone needs.

You built your audience on video. But when someone clicks the link in your bio, they land on a linktree — a page designed for a phone screen, because that's all it was ever built for. A few buttons, no story, nothing that shows what you actually do or why you can help.

The other option is worse. A full website has more pages than anyone will click through, when all a prospect really wants is the highlights — with the option to go deeper if they're interested. And almost none of them have room for the video that brought that visitor there in the first place.

WHY I BUILT THIS

Why I Built Shelly Sites

I kept running into the same gap. Linktree-style pages look great on a phone and fall apart the second someone opens them on a laptop — because they were never designed for a browser, just a thumb. And they don't say enough. A visitor lands, sees a stack of buttons, and has no sense of the problem you solve, the proof you can solve it, or what working with you actually looks like.

Full custom websites solve the "not enough information" problem by overcorrecting — ten, twenty pages of content a prospect has to dig through to find the three things they actually came for.

Shelly Sites sits in the middle.

One site, built to work on mobile and browser, that tells your story in the order a prospect actually needs it: the problem they have, the proof you can help, the promise of what you deliver, and the plan to get there. Same structure every time, because that structure is what makes people act — but the design, colors, and content are built around your brand, not a template you have to force your business into.
