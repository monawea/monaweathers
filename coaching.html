How The Build works

The Build runs for 8 weeks in three phases. You get three 1:1 calls with me (kickoff, midpoint, and wrap-up), plus weekly group coaching calls in The Growth Circle from day one.

  1. Ducks in a Rowabout 2 weeks
    We start with your kickoff call. I audit your business, get clear on your offer and ideal client, clean up your tech, and choose the pieces you need most. You fill out one intake form so I have everything I need to build. By the end of week 2, you get your Build Map: a one-page picture of your plan, with the pieces you chose, what gets built when, what "done" looks like for each one, and your launch and call dates.
  2. Build Your Piecesabout 4 weeks
    We build the pieces you chose side by side. At the midpoint call, we review your progress, you approve your work, and we adjust the plan if needed.
  3. Finish and Launchabout 2 weeks
    We test every link, form, and email, and everything goes live. On your wrap-up call, we review what you built and map your next 90 days.
