How the year works

You can join any time after your Build wrap-up call. There are no start dates and no waiting for a group to fill. Everyone starts with the same first step, and then we move at your pace.

  1. Months 1–2 Design Your Program
    Map your stages, create your paths, define what your clients walk away with, and set your price.
  2. Months 3–5 Connect
    Conversations, follow-up, and community, all pointing to your program.
  3. Months 6–8 Convert
    Sales calls and an offer presentation for the program you designed.
  4. Months 9–12 Activate and Refine
    Templates built from real client work, numbers reviewed, delivery tightened, and prices raised.
